Rulebook · PayPal sandbox · NEAR testnet

We built a verifiable rulebook for agentic payments.

The rules live in a public NEAR contract that anyone can read, and every refusal is a transaction anyone can check. The agent never holds a PayPal key.

From 2013 to 2015, Google and Facebook paid over USD 100 million to a fake supplier with a real company's name and real-looking invoices (US DOJ press release; the companies confirmed they were the victims). Now imagine an AI agent paying your invoices.

The contract enforces

allowed payees or merchants, the per-payment cap, the budget per period, a validity window of at most 5 minutes per signed order, pay-once per request_id, and revoke.

The AI scam check

reads each invoice, listing or ticket and can hold it for the owner. It can only hold, never loosen the rulebook: a fooled AI still can't pay a lookalike.
